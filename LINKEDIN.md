## Headline (≤ ~80 characters)

**TraceRail — delay-risk maintenance for metro fleets (CBM demo)**

## Short description (profile / project body)

I built a demo SaaS that turns raw vehicle measurements into a ranked fleet view and a maintainer-ready work line—before a door or brake holds the timetable. Controllers send amps, times, and pressures; TraceRail scores health and delay minutes at risk, shows the formula on the page, and drafts tonight’s action for human accept or edit. FastAPI, SQLite, optional LLM. Synthetic Nordic Metro only—built to explain cloud, API, data, and automation in pre-sales conversations.

## Highlights (one paragraph or separate lines)

- Fleet ranked by **delay minutes at risk**, aligned to how operators feel failures.  
- **Side-view train**: click doors, brakes, HVAC, or traction; maintenance readiness loads from stored telemetry.  
- **Transparent scoring**: failure probability × typical delay—visible, not a black box.  
- **Human gate**: depot engineer accepts or edits the draft; decision persisted in the database.  
- **Integration story**: OpenAPI for TCMS / depot ingest (`/docs`).
